Delete your SplitWiser account

In the app (instant)

  1. Open SplitWiser and go to Account.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account and confirm.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or can't sign in, send a deletion request (it is public, so don't include your email address, password or any other private details). We'll reply there with a private way to confirm the account is yours, then delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login, email address and password
  • Your phone number, profile photo and avatar
  • Your friend list and your comments
  • Your devices, push notification tokens and notification settings

What stays

Expenses and payments you shared with others stay in their groups and history so their balances remain correct. Your name on them is replaced with "Deleted user", and nothing in them links back to you.
