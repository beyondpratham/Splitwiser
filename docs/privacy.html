SplitWiser

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

SplitWiser is a free app for splitting bills with friends. It is made by Pratham, an independent developer in India, and its source code is public on GitHub. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

In short
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell or rent your information.
  • We only keep what the app needs to work: your account, the expenses you share, and your settings.
  • Your phone number is never shown to anyone.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time.

What we collect and why

InformationWhy
Name, email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash; nobody can read them.
Phone number (optional)So people who already have your number can find you when they sync their contacts. You can switch this off in Settings → Privacy. Your number is never shown to other people.
Profile photo or avatar (optional)Shown next to your name to friends and group members. Photos are shrunk on your phone before upload.
Friends, groups, expenses, payments, comments and notesThis is the app's purpose: keeping track of who owes whom. They are shared only with the people involved.
A random device IDYour account works on one device at a time; this tells the app which device is the active one.
Push notification token (if you allow notifications)To deliver the notifications you chose.
Notification and privacy settingsTo remember your choices.

Phone permissions

PermissionHow it's used
ContactsOnly when you tap Sync contacts. The email addresses and phone numbers in your contacts are sent securely to our server once, only to check which of them already use SplitWiser. They are not stored, and nobody is contacted.
CameraTo scan a friend's QR code (the image never leaves your phone) or to take a profile photo.
PhotosOnly the single picture you choose as your profile photo.
NotificationsTo show the alerts you turned on.
Fingerprint / face unlockFor the optional app lock. Your phone checks it; SplitWiser never receives any biometric data.

SplitWiser does not use your location, your advertising ID, or any analytics or advertising services. Payments such as "Buy me a coffee" happen in your own UPI app; SplitWiser never sees your bank or card details.

Who can see your information

  • Friends and group members see your name, email address, avatar, and the expenses, payments and comments you share with them.
  • Nobody else can see your information. Every request is checked on the server, so people can only read what is shared with them.
  • We (the developer) can access the database to run and fix the service, and do so only when needed.

Services that help run SplitWiser

These companies process information only to provide their part of the service:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server hosting.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.
  • Resend: sending emails, such as password reset codes and the notification emails you choose.

Their servers may be outside your country. Information is always encrypted while it travels between your phone and these services.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your information while your account exists. To delete it, open Settings → Account → Delete account in the app. Your login, email, phone number, photo, friend list, comments, device and notification data are removed straight away. Expenses you shared stay for the other people in them, so their balances stay correct, but they show "Deleted user" instead of your name.

If you can't use the app, see how to request deletion.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS encryption. Passwords are hashed, and database rules make sure each person can only reach their own information and what others shared with them. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell affected users about any breach that puts it at risk.

Children

SplitWiser is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can see and change your profile in the app, and delete your account at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your information or ask questions about how it is handled, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 or other laws that apply where you live.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Important changes will also be announced in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on GitHub.
